A make.fun entry for [Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9): one kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, in 120 characters or fewer.

`main.ts` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Soon, a tall stranger will bring you water — and remember the second cup. Your leaves will stand a little taller.

Run it with Node 22: `node main.ts`.
